# Six-path affinity systems

## Key lessons

- **Make the limit the source of power.** In Nen, a stricter self-imposed vow makes an ability stronger, and breaking it is costly. Tie our "strong but restricted" abilities to the off-path penalty. Source: [Epic Stream on Gon's vow](https://epicstream.com/article/will-gon-get-his-nen-back-in-hunter-x-hunter) (snippet only).
- **Count progress by a rule that favors focus.** Oblivion's original level-up counted only Major skills. Off-path progress should be real but slower. Source: [UESP Oblivion Efficient Leveling](https://content1.uesp.net/wiki/Oblivion:Efficient_Leveling) (snippet only).
- **Open the tree, charge by distance.** Path of Exile uses one shared tree; the class sets the start, and any node is reachable for enough points. Source: [PoE passive skill wiki](https://pathofexile.fandom.com/wiki/Passive_skill).
- **Design the starting position.** FFX put everyone on one grid; the default layout felt linear, and players wanted a better start. Source: [GameSkinny, FFX grids](https://www.gameskinny.com/tips/final-fantasy-x-standard-sphere-grid-vs-expert-sphere-grid/).
- **Use counters as bias, not lock.** RuneScape's combat triangle gives an edge but the wiki says it does not decide fights. Source: [OSRS Wiki, Combat Triangle](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Combat_Triangle).
- **Treat "one best type" claims as a test flag.** A fan d20 homebrew calls Enhancement the best balance. Test it in PvP. Source: [GiantITP d20 Nen thread](https://forums.giantitp.com/archive/index.php/t-503437.html) (snippet only).
- **Paid rerolls need disclosed odds.** Roblox requires numerical odds for randomized items bought with Robux or in-game currency, including indirect purchases. Source: [Roblox Creator Hub, Paid random items](https://create.roblox.com/docs/en-us/production/monetization/paid-random-items) (snippet only).
- **Fan Nen games sell rerolls through codes.** Source: [Pocket Tactics, Eternal Nen codes](https://www.pockettactics.com/eternal-nen/codes).
- **Investment thresholds gate upgrades.** Deepwoken's attunement needs 20-point steps for mantra upgrades, and players warn that low investment hurts damage. Source: [LDPlayer Deepwoken guide](https://www.ldplayer.net/blog/roblox-deepwoken-build-guide.html) (snippet only).

## Source structure summary

- **Categories:** Enhancement, Transmutation, Emission, Conjuration, Manipulation, Specialization. One German guide says "Materialization" for the fourth; unconfirmed. Source: [Sportskeeda](https://www.sportskeeda.com/anime/i-don-t-think-hunter-x-hunter-commended-enough-power-system-needs-change) (snippet only).
- **Hexagon and efficiency:** Own type 100%, adjacent 80%, next two 60%, opposite 40%. The 60% figure appears in one source only.
- **Type assignment:** A water divination test. Sources agree on some results and conflict on Emitter and Enhancer. Source: [Beebom Nen chart](https://beebom.com/hunter-x-hunter-nen-chart-explained/amp/) (snippet only).
- **Conditions:** Vows are self-imposed rules. Stricter vows give stronger abilities; breaking one has a serious cost.

## Design options for us

1. **XP multiplier on off-path progress (e.g. 100/80/60/40%).**
   - Pros: simple, keeps every path open, easy to tune.
   - Cons: far paths may be ignored and useless in PvP unless payoff is strong.
2. **Level cap on off-path abilities.**
   - Pros: clear rule; natural paths stay strong.
   - Cons: feels punitive; capped paths may still be weak in PvP.
3. **Skill slot cost for off-path abilities.**
   - Pros: loadout choices matter in PvP; matches the "limit equals power" idea.
   - Cons: adds UI and slot scarcity; needs a respec rule.

# M7: Endgame and Launch — Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** The Ledger reaches 50 Relics, with three Crown Relics behind one unique challenge each (a world boss, a round-the-island course, a contested 10-second hold on a throne); claiming every Relic Crowns the player with a server-wide ceremony, a title over the head, a code-built circlet, an optional Roblox badge and a place on the all-time board on the Tally Stone in Tallyport; a Crowned player can Renounce there, after a typed confirm, to return every card for a permanent star and start again; and two launch fixes land (the game's own health regeneration, server-checked cache holds).

**Architecture:** As in M1–M6. Every rule is a pure Luau module in `src/shared/Rules`, unit-tested with Lune: `Crown` (the saved record, one Crown per run, titles), `Renounce` (refusals, the two-step confirm, what is returned), `Rounds` (the two checklists), `Leaderboard` (store pages, merging, board text, when to write), `Regen`, and `Sources.holdOk`. Thin server services own state: `CrownService` (Crowning, ceremony, title, circlet, badge, Renounce), `LeaderboardService` (the one background thread that talks to the ordered data store and draws the Tally Stone), `RegenService`. Content is data: the 20 Relics, the Gilded Bailiff, four trials, two rounds and every new place are catalog entries.

**Tech Stack:** Luau (`--!strict`), Rojo 7.7.1, Lune 0.11.1, StyLua 2.6.0 (Luau build), Selene 0.31.0, ProfileStore 1.0.3 (vendored), plain Roblox UI instances, `DataStoreService:GetOrderedDataStore`, `BadgeService`.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-10-isle-of-avarice-design.md` revision 9 — section 3.15 (all M7 values and rulings), with 3.1, 3.2, 3.4, 3.8, 3.9–3.14 and the Wide Isle spec's sections 4–6 (regions, hazards, `Geography.place`).

**Branch:** `feat/m7-endgame`, based on `feat/m6-parties` after M6's last commit.

## Global Constraints

- Original content only. Tone as before: "defeated", never "killed". The words players see: "Crowned", "Renounce", "star", "Tally Stone", "The Crowned", "Gilded Bailiff", "The Grand Tally", "The Warden's Round", "The Beast Tally". No paid items.
- Every file under `src/` except `src/server/Vendor` starts with `--!strict`. Files under `src/shared/` use no Roblox APIs and require each other only by relative string. Rule functions never mutate their arguments (exception: `RateLimiter.allow`). Selene's standard library here does not know `Random`: use `math.random`.
- The server decides everything. A request names at most a word or a kind; the server checks who, where, when and whether they may. No yielding call between reading a Ledger, Vault, Coin, crown record, rounds record or trial record and writing it back.
- Card moves are atomic (spec 5.3 rule 3): Renounce writes the Ledger, Vault, crown record, trial record and rounds record in one step with no yield between.
- Ledger changes go through `LedgerService.set` (Vault through `LedgerService.setVault`), Coin through `SourceService.addCoin`, reward Relics through `SourceService.award` (or `Payout.give`), XP through `PathService.grant`.
- RemoteEvents are created with `LedgerService.makeRemote(name)` and named in `src/shared/Net.luau`; message codes live in `src/shared/Catalog/Messages.luau`.
- **Data store and badge calls** (`GetOrderedDataStore`, `UpdateAsync`, `GetSortedAsync`, `GetNameFromUserIdAsync`, `AwardBadge`, `UserHasBadgeAsync`): each inside `pcall`; only in `LeaderboardService`'s one background thread or in a `task.spawn` from `CrownService`; never in a remote handler, never on a player's load path, never between reading and writing player data. After such a call yields, the only write back to saved data is `crown.boardCrowns` or `crown.badgeAwarded`, after reading `DataService.get(player)` again.
- Saved data stays `schemaVersion = 1`: two additive fields, `crown` and `rounds`, each with its own sanitize on load (as `trials` did in M4).
- **Spec 3.15 values:**
  - Relics 031–034 Copper, 035–040 Silver, 041–045 Gold, 046–047 Platinum, 048–050 Crown; totals Copper 13, Silver 15, Gold 15, Platinum 4, Crown 3.
  - Gilded Bailiff: 2400 health, Gavel Slam 30 damage to every player within 12 studs every 3.5 s, Lunge 20 studs / 25 damage / every 9 s, speed 12, sight 50, 900 XP, 120 Coin, respawn 1200 s, drops R048 1.0 and R041 0.3. New drops: Brinehound R035 0.06, Shellback R036 0.08, Bogspitter R037 0.08, Brine Colossus R043 0.15.
  - Trials: Reed Run (course, 6 markers, 70 s, R040, 40 Coin, 170 XP), The Bellows Dance (course, 5 markers, 50 s, R042, 90, 400), Spitter Season (hunt 3 Bogspitters, 180 s, R045, 80, 380), The Grand Tally (course, 7 markers, 300 s, R049, 200, 900).
  - Rounds: The Warden's Round (the nine trials other than The Grand Tally; R046, 250 Coin, 800 XP), The Beast Tally (brinehound, shellback, bogspitter, brine_colossus, gilded_bailiff; R047, 250 Coin, 800 XP).
  - Edict Hall stock gains R031 at 40 Coin. Cache holds: C029 3 s, C031 10 s; `Sources.HOLD_SLACK = 0.3`.
  - One Crown per run (`crowns` may rise only while `crowns == stars`). A complete Ledger's claimed copies do not count against caps.
  - Board: ordered data store `CrownedBoard_v1`, key `tostring(userId)`, value = Crowns; 10 rows; read every 120 s and 5 s after a successful write; a failed write retried for that player no sooner than 60 s later; writes never lower a stored count.
  - Renounce: prompt hold 2 s, offer 60 s, word "RENOUNCE" (trimmed, any case, at most 32 characters); fight window 30 s.
  - Regeneration: +1 health per second, at most 1 s per step, only 10 s after the last loss of health; ticked every 0.5 s.
- `bash scripts/check.sh` passes before every commit (StyLua, Selene, Rojo build, Lune tests). Nothing in it type-checks Luau or runs Roblox APIs: services and UI are proven only by the Studio play test, so read server and client code twice.

## Review Focus

1. **Renounce is irreversible.** It must be refused in every unsafe state (not Crowned this run, incomplete, defeated, locked by a claim, making a claim, trading, in a trial, outside Tallyport, Marked, in a fight), need a live offer and the typed word, re-check everything at the confirm, survive hostile `RenounceAction` payloads, and write all five parts with no yield. Pinned in Task 2 (`Renounce.check`, `parse`, `confirm`, `apply`), Task 6 (handler order, by reading) and the play test (M7.6–M7.8).
2. **One Crown per run, and no free Crowns from bad data.** Unclaim-and-reclaim, rejoining, two quick Ledger writes and corrupt saved records can never add a Crown; stars stay `crowns` or `crowns − 1`; an unreadable star count reads as "already Crowned this run". Pinned in Task 2 (`Crown.sanitize`, `canCrown`, `crown`), Task 5 (`tryCrown` has no yield between check and write) and the play test (M7.4).
3. **Data stores and badges never hurt play.** Every call wrapped and off the hot path as in Global Constraints; the board still draws (offline) when the store fails; writes only raise; a failed write backs off 60 s; Studio's unpublished place leaves the game fully playable. Pinned in Task 3 (`Leaderboard.fromPage`, `merge`, `shouldWrite`, `keep`), Task 4 (by reading `LeaderboardService`), Task 5 (badge thread) and the play test (M7.5 with a stand-in backend and a failing one).
4. **Scarcity at 50 and the Crowns.** Every new source obeys the caps; each Crown seals at 1 and reopens when its holder Renounces, leaves or completes their Ledger; a complete Ledger's claimed copies are not counted but its spares are; held caches open only after a real hold. Pinned in Task 1 (catalog and world tests), Task 2 (`Scarcity.countHeld`), Task 3 (`Sources.holdOk`, `tryCollect`) and the play test (M7.2, M7.3, M7.12).
5. **30 becomes 50 everywhere.** Every test, UI, driver check and document that pinned 30 Relics, 24 caches, 25 creature slots, 6 trials, 14 markers or the 6 × 5 claim grid is updated, and nothing else drifts. Pinned in Task 1 (the full test list), Task 8 (claim grid, book), Task 9 (driver counts and the checklists).
